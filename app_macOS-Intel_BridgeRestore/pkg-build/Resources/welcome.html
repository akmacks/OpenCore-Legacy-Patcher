Welcome to Bridge Restore v1.0.2-alpha

A Thunderbolt Bridge connectivity suite for Intel non-T2 Macs running macOS Sonoma, Sequoia, or Tahoe.

What will be installed

  • BridgeRestore.app — GUI + CLI launcher in /Applications
  • CLI commands in /usr/local/bin: bridge-restore, tunnel-mini, tunnel-mini-status, bridge-restore-gui
  • Scripts in ~/Library/Application Support/BridgeRestore/scripts/
  • LaunchAgents — watchdog, NAT persistence, tunnel automation
  • Man page — man bridge-restore
  • NAT anchor — /etc/pf.anchors/bridge-restore

Requirements

  • Intel Mac (x86_64) — not compatible with Apple Silicon
  • macOS 14.0 (Sonoma) or later
  • Thunderbolt 2/3 cable for bridge connection
  • Passwordless sudo recommended (/etc/sudoers.d/yourname-nopasswd)
Upgrading? The installer will detect your existing version and offer to archive it before installing. Archives are saved to ~/Library/Application Support/BridgeRestore/archives/.

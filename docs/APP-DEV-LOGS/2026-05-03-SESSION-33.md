## 📋 Executive Summary

Mac mini had been failing to boot for 36+ hours with a hang at ~1/3 progress bar.
Session 32 misdiagnosed the cause as `amfi_get_out_of_my_way=0x7ff` being too aggressive.
Real cause: `BootKernelExtensions.kc` in the Preboot volume was corrupted by a shutdown
stall at 23:09 on April 25 (which interrupted a macOS software update process writing to
Preboot). Fix: replaced Preboot KC with the original stock KC from the sealed System volume
(March 20 timestamp). Old KC backed up. Verbose boot (`-v`) added to OC boot-args.
Mini ejected from TDM — boot test in progress.

## 🔬 Detailed Findings

### Finding 1 — Mini DID Boot After Session 31 (Session 32 diagnosis was wrong)
- **Evidence:** Multiple `softwareupdated` .ips crash reports: Apr 25 at 15:49, 17:58,
  19:19 and Apr 26 at 10:38 in DiagnosticReports
- **Conclusion:** Mini booted fine after Session 31's bless reboot; KC was functional
- **Implication:** Session 32's AMFI diagnosis was incorrect — not the root cause

### Finding 2 — Shutdown Stall at 23:09 Apr 25 Was the True Culprit
- **File:** `shutdown_stall_2026-04-25-230932_Mac-mini-Server-i7.shutdownStall` (894KB)
- **Theory:** softwareupdated (already crashing repeatedly) was writing to Preboot when
  the stall occurred, leaving `BootKernelExtensions.kc` corrupted/partially written
- **Result:** All subsequent boots hang at ~1/3 (kext loading phase stall, not panic)

### Finding 3 — MBP and Mini on Different Tahoe Builds — kmutil Rebuild Not Possible
- **MBP:** macOS 26.5 (25F5068a) | **Mini:** macOS 26.4 (25E246)
- Cannot use `kmutil install --volume-root` from MBP — would produce wrong-version KC

### Finding 4 — System Volume Has Original Stock KC (the fix source)
- **Path:** `/Volumes/Server HD/System/Library/KernelCollections/BootKernelExtensions.kc`
- **Timestamp:** March 20, 15:25 — original OS installation KC, sealed and verified
- **Size:** 64MB | Magic: `cffaedfe07000001` (valid Mach-O 64-bit)
- Also present: `SystemKernelExtensions.kc` (345MB) — not needed in Preboot

### Finding 5 — Preboot Volume Writable from TDM, APFS Integrity Sound
- Mount flags: `noowners` — ownership checks bypassed, writes permitted
- `diskutil verifyVolume disk12s2` exit 0 — APFS structure intact

### Finding 6 — No Kernel Panic Files
- DiagnosticReports: only `updater-*.ips` and `shutdown_stall_*.shutdownStall`
- No `.panic` files — boot hang is a stall/deadlock, not a kernel panic
- `-nokcmismatchpanic` suppresses the panic signal but does not resolve
  the underlying deadlock when the KC is truly corrupted

### Finding 7 — /Library/Extensions Contains Third-Party Kexts (not the issue)
- AppleALC.kext, Lilu.kext, RestrictEvents.kext (Apr 13) — dual-present in OC EFI too
- AuxKC (`Library/KernelCollections/` in Preboot) is empty — OC injection only, no conflict
